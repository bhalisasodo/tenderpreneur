# Brand Identity Brief — BoQPro

## 0. Name: Decided

**BoQPro** is the final name. It's literal (BoQ = Bill of Quantities, the core document the platform works from) and reads as enterprise tooling — closer to Sage, Xero, or QuickBooks naming conventions than a consumer app. That's a strength for the institutional trust positioning: it tells a supply chain officer exactly what the tool does before they've clicked anything, and it doesn't carry any of the "Tenderpreneur" baggage. The trade-off is it's less distinctive as a brand — the visual identity and voice need to carry more of the differentiation since the name itself won't.

## 1. Context

A BoQ pricing & supplier marketplace platform for South African tenderpreneurs — the first build phase of a broader bid-to-delivery workflow tool (BoQ parsing, benchmark pricing, supplier sourcing, eventually delivery/compliance and funding-readiness tooling). Working name so far: "Tenderpreneur." Tone target: **serious and institutional** — this handles bid pricing and compliance, so it needs to read as trustworthy and credible to someone about to commit real money on a government tender, not as a scrappy startup toy.

---

## 2. Positioning

**Positioning statement:**
For South African SMEs bidding on public and private tenders, BoQPro is the pricing and sourcing platform that turns a Bill of Quantities into an accurate, defensible, competitively-priced bid — because winning a tender is only the start; pricing it wrong is how you lose the contract before you've begun.

**Core promise:** Precision and defensibility. Every price the platform surfaces should feel like something you could stand behind in front of a bid adjudication committee.

**Category framing:** Not "tender help" (associated with consultants and shortcuts) — but **bid-pricing infrastructure**, positioned closer to how accounting or compliance software is framed than how coaching/advisory services are framed.

---

## 3. Voice & Messaging Principles

- **Precise over persuasive.** Lead with numbers, methodology, and data provenance (where a benchmark price comes from) rather than motivational language. Institutional trust is earned through specificity, not enthusiasm.
- **Plain language, formal register.** Avoid hype words ("revolutionize," "unlock," "game-changer"). Avoid slang. Write the way a competent quantity surveyor would write an email — direct, unembellished, correct.
- **Show the work.** Wherever possible, messaging should reference process (two-pass classification, confidence scoring, human review) rather than just outcomes — this is a compliance-adjacent product, and visible rigor is the trust signal.
- **Respect the stakes.** Never joke about losing a tender, cash flow, or compliance risk — these are livelihoods. Humor, if any, stays in onboarding/empty-states, never in pricing or compliance copy.

---

## 4. Visual Identity Direction

**Overall impression to aim for:** something that could sit next to a bank's business-banking product or a municipal SCM portal without looking out of place — not a consumer app, not a "startup" aesthetic.

- **Color palette:** Deep navy or charcoal as the primary (authority, stability) with a single confident accent — a deep gold/ochre works well for South African institutional contexts (echoes currency, official documents, government seals) without tipping into gaudy. Avoid bright SaaS-gradient palettes (purple/teal gradients read as consumer-tech, undermines the compliance tone).
- **Typography:** A serif or slab-serif for wordmark/headings (signals institutional weight — think banking, legal, insurance branding) paired with a clean grotesk sans for UI/body text (keeps the product itself usable and modern). Avoid rounded, friendly sans-serifs across the board — they read as approachable-startup, not institutional.
- **Logo mark:** Finalized as a rounded check-square — a rounded-square outline with a bold checkmark inside, paired with the "BoQPro" wordmark set in a serif at 500 weight. Chosen over an earlier folded-document concept because it stays legible at small sizes (16px favicon/app icon) and reproduces more reliably under low-quality reproduction (fax, photocopy, stamp) — both real constraints for this audience. The folded-document mark is kept as a secondary illustrative asset for larger-format use (website, print) where its extra detail can register.
- **Color values:** Navy `#12233F` (primary/authority), gold `#D9A94A` (accent, on-dark), darker gold `#96731F` (accent, on-light — for AA contrast on the light-background variant).
- **Lockup variants:** Horizontal (icon left, wordmark right) is the primary lockup for headers, letterhead, and email signature. Stacked (icon above wordmark) is the fallback for square placements. Icon-only is the app icon / favicon. A light-background reversed variant (navy icon+text on cream `#F4F1EA`) covers printed/white-background use. A one-color monochrome variant (navy only, no gold) covers black-and-white reproduction.
- **Logo concept direction:** Favor a wordmark or a simple geometric mark over an illustrative/mascot logo. If a mark is used, geometric forms that suggest structure, ledgers, or measurement (a grid, a checkmark integrated into a document shape, a balanced scale) reinforce the "precision and compliance" positioning better than anything organic or playful.
- **Imagery style (marketing/website):** Real construction sites, real documents, real people in hi-vis and hardhats reviewing paperwork — not stock-photo handshakes or abstract tech illustrations. Authenticity to the actual bid/construction context matters more than polish.

## 6. Tagline Options

All aim for the same register as the voice principles above — precise, unembellished, no hype words. Grouped by angle:

**Precision/defensibility angle** (closest to the core promise)
- *Price it right. Win it clean.*
- *Every price, defensible.*
- *Bid with numbers you can stand behind.*

**Function-first angle** (literal, safest, matches the name's directness)
- *Bill of Quantities. Priced properly.*
- *Turn your BoQ into a bid.*
- *Pricing built for the bid, not the guess.*

**Outcome/stakes angle** (names the cost of getting it wrong, without hype)
- *Because a wrong price loses the tender before it's read.*
- *Win the tender. Keep the margin.*

**Chosen tagline:** *Price it right. Win it clean.*
